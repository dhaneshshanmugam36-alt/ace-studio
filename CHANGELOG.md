## 2.0.6 · Film camera
- **Ace Looks:** Film camera with live film looks, tap to focus, pinch to zoom and lens switching
- Night mode merges 8 frames to cut noise; Smart HDR brings out shadows
- Every camera shot saves with the look and as a clean original
- "Use my phone's camera app" button

- 10 new looks (32 in total): Portra 160, Superia 400, Ultramax 400, ColorPlus 200, Agfa Vista 200,
